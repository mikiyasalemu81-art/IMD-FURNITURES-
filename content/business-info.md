## Our Story
Founded in Addis Ababa with an uncompromising devotion to master craftsmanship, IMD Furniture & Design elevates Ethiopian living spaces through timeless, bespoke furniture. Every piece begins with sustainably sourced hardwood, shaped with millimeter precision and finished by hand.

From our showroom at Century Mall to homes, embassies, and luxury estates across Addis Ababa, we reject mass production in favor of heirloom distinction. We believe furniture should carry emotional resonance, enduring beauty, and supreme comfort.

Each commission is an intimate collaboration between client and master artisan. Whether designing an imposing dining centerpiece or an intimate reading lounge, we tailor dimensions, timber species, and tactile textiles to realize your unique architectural vision.
